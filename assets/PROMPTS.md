# BETWEEN image generation

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Thirteen photographic masters; no text rendered into the images. Original PNGs are preserved in `assets/originals/`. Delivery AVIF/WebP files are generated with Sharp.

## Shared direction

High-end, photorealistic cinematic editorial photography. Deep warm charcoal #111110, ivory, muted amber #DDA56F. Fine analog grain, natural skin pores and human imperfections, sculptural side light. Intimate, restrained, quietly hopeful. No text, logos, watermarks, borders, neon, glowing eyes, plastic skin, or panel collages.

Recurring woman: young adult, olive freckled skin, dark eyebrows, dark brown hair pulled back, black sleeveless clothing. Recurring man: young adult, warm brown skin, short dark curls, clean shaven, simple black clothing. Preserve identities across references. Human connection is expressed through attention and shared light.

## Prompt set

1. **within.png**, landscape 16:9. Very close three-quarter portrait of the woman on the right, eyes gently closed, chin slightly raised. Left 48% remains dark for typography. Soft amber light crosses forehead, cheek, nose, and throat. Subtle golden optical streak at the far right. Reference for the whole visual world.
2. **thought.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Within. Same portrait reflected through two large imperfect translucent glass sheets, partial reflections of eye, cheek, and profile at separate depths. Main portrait occupies right half; left stays dark. Smoked amber glass, optical distortion, restrained double exposure, no sharp shards or horror.
3. **connection.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Within. Woman left, man right, in calm mutual eye contact. Profile portraits separated by deep central space. A thin organic amber light ribbon travels between their chests, illuminating both faces. Black clothing fades into darkness; natural skin remains visible.
4. **encounter.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Connection. Wider and more distant framing; same two people on opposite sides, gazing across darkness. Gentle amber rim light. No connecting ribbon yet. Anticipation before contact; dark upper/lower space for typography.
5. **afterglow.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Connection. Same pair shoulder to shoulder on the right, woman slightly forward, both looking toward a warm horizon off-frame right. Waist-up, quietly hopeful, no romantic posing. Left half remains dark.
6. **connection-mobile.png**, portrait 9:16. Reference Connection. Upper 42% dark for typography; full profile faces visible on opposite sides in the lower portion. Eyes around 58% of height. Ribbon at chest height around 76%. Both shoulders remain in frame.
7. **encounter-mobile.png**, portrait 9:16. Reference Connection. No ribbon. Both full faces visible at upper-middle, facing each other. Lower 38% dark for typography, upper edge also dark. Preserve warm rim light and anticipation.
8. **afterglow-mobile.png**, portrait 9:16. Reference Afterglow. Both full heads fit across upper half, centered around 28% height, woman slightly forward. Both look toward the same golden horizon. Shoulders fade into dark lower 50%, leaving space for final typography.

9. **touch.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Connection for skin tones and amber lighting. Extreme close-up of two adult hands, olive freckled skin from the left and warm brown skin from the right. Index fingertips almost meet at the center, separated by a small gap. Fine pores and body hairs, relaxed fingers, sculptural amber sidelight, deep charcoal negative space. No graphics or literal glowing effects.
10. **iris.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference the woman in Connection. Extreme close-up of one open hazel eye at the right two-thirds of the frame. Natural radiating iris fibers and soft rectangular light reflection. Sharp lashes, freckles, visible pores; left half falls into deep shadow. Amber macro photography, no artificial stars or science-fiction effects.
11. **iris-detail.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Iris. A genuinely closer photographic composition with the iris nearly half the frame height. Reconstruct natural iris fibers, eyelashes, and pores; keep hazel color and warm lighting. Replaces extreme digital enlargement during the opening scroll.
12. **connection-enhanced.png**, landscape 16:9. Reference Connection. Preserve subjects, framing, and light ribbon; refine eyelashes, pores, curls, and facial hair. Reduce muddy texture. Used as the desktop Connection source; the earlier master remains preserved.
13. **iris-mobile.png**, portrait 9:16. Reference Iris. New native portrait composition with the eye near the upper-middle and the lower third fading into charcoal. Preserves fine eye detail without enlarging a narrow landscape crop. Also supplies the mobile detail transition.

Within and Thought mobile exports use deliberate right-side crops. Iris, Encounter, Connection, and Afterglow have separately generated mobile compositions. Touch retains both hands in a wider composition, with CSS feathering into the mobile background. The generator returned native 1672×941 landscapes and 941×1672 portraits; no interpolated master is described as 4K.
